Privacy Panda Coloring Sheets

Learn about digital privacy while having fun coloring. Each sheet is sized for US Letter (8.5" × 11") printing.

1. Privacy Panda - Basic

Color Privacy Panda as he protects the treasure chest with a strong password lock!

Privacy Tip: Passwords are like special keys to your digital home. Keep them secret and make them strong!

2. Privacy Shield

Design your own privacy protection shield!

Privacy Tip: Never share your full name, address, phone number, or school name with strangers online.

3. Password Treasure Chest

Color the treasure chest that keeps passwords safe!

Privacy Tip: Always ask a trusted adult before clicking on links or downloading anything.

4. Digital Footprint Map

Trace your digital journey and learn about online privacy!

Privacy Tip: Good friends respect your privacy and never ask you to share personal information.

5. Privacy Garden

Beautiful garden scene with privacy-themed elements!

Privacy Tip: The internet is like a big forest — it is fun to explore, but always stay on the safe paths!

6. Cyber Safety Scene

Complete scene showing safe and unsafe online behaviors!

Privacy Tip: Always think before you click and ask a trusted adult if you are unsure!

Ready to color?

Use your browser print dialog (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and choose Letter paper with fit to page off for best line clarity.
