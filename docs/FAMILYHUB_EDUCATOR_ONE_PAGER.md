# PandaGarde Family Hub — Educator Brief (one page + standards table)

**For K–12 teachers, librarians, and counselors · Ages 5–17 · Send-home digital citizenship practice**

PandaGarde is **not** a classroom LMS or student monitoring tool. Family Hub is a **parent-guided** app: short missions with a real scenario, activity, family conversation, and **one concrete action** (settings change, house rule, opt-out step). Progress stays on the family’s device.

Use this brief to pair **in-class lessons** (stories, discussion, printables on [pandagarde.com](https://www.pandagarde.com)) with **at-home follow-through** (Family Hub missions).

---

## Classroom → home model (15-minute prep)

| Step | In school (you) | At home (family) |
|------|-----------------|------------------|
| **1. Frame** | Introduce the topic with a Privacy Panda story or classroom activity from Guides & stories | — |
| **2. Assign** | Share the matching mission name + age band (table below) | Parent and child open **Family Hub** (app or `hub.pandagarde.com`) |
| **3. Close the loop** | Optional: “What rule or setting did your family change?” exit ticket | Mission ends with **one real fix** — no account or cloud sync required |

**Honest limits:** No teacher dashboard, no roster sync, no proof of completion beyond what families choose to share. Pilot schools can collect voluntary exit tickets or parent surveys for evidence.

## Standards framework used

| Framework | How PandaGarde uses it |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **ISTE Standards for Students (2016)** | Primary reference for district curriculum maps — indicators **2a–2e** (Digital Citizen) dominate; see table |
| **Common Sense Education — Digital Citizenship** | Topic tags for librarians and CS-aligned pacing |
| **PandaGarde Five Quest Pillars** | Internal canon (`docs/STORYLINE_BIBLE.md`) — Connection, Memory, Protection, Creation, Wisdom |

> ISTE published refreshed student standards in 2024. Districts still on 2016 maps can use this table as-is; refreshed mappings can be added when your LEA adopts ISTE 2024.

### ISTE Digital Citizen quick reference

| Indicator | Student outcome (summary) |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| **2a** | Manage digital identity and reputation |
| **2b** | Engage in positive, safe, legal, ethical online behavior |
| **2c** | Manage personal data; maintain privacy and security |
| **2d** | Understand societal impacts of technology |
| **2e** | Advocate for equitable access and digital rights |

## Suggested pacing by grade band

### Elementary (K–5) — missions 1–6

| Week theme | Teach in class | Send home (Family Hub) |
|------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| What stays private? | Story + sorting activity | Pack Your Digital Backpack |

## What to tell families (copy-paste)

> **Family Hub homework:** After our class lesson on [topic], please complete the mission **“[Mission name]”** in the PandaGarde Family Hub app (or at hub.pandagarde.com). It takes about [5–18] minutes. You’ll talk together and change one real setting or agree on one family rule. Progress stays on your device — nothing is uploaded to school.

## Policy & product truth (for administrators)

| Claim | Accurate? |
|-------|-----------|
| Teaches digital citizenship & privacy skills | **Yes** |
| Replaces parental controls or school MDM | **No** |
| Monitors children’s devices or messages | **No** |
| Cloud roster or teacher gradebook | **No** (v1) |
| Collects student PII on PandaGarde servers | **No** — local device storage only |
| Aligns with family empowerment framing (e.g. KIDS Act Title V education goals) | **Thematic** — see `docs/KIDS_ACT_ALIGNMENT.md` |
